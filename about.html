Hi there, I’m Ethan

Hi, I’m Ethan, a game developer from the Niagara Region, and thanks for stopping by my portfolio! I love making games. It’s been a passion of mine for years, and I’m lucky to have two of them out on Steam now: a goofy office sim and an award-winning party brawler. I sharpened my craft in the dual-degree GAME Programming program at Brock University and Niagara College.

My focus is Gameplay Programming, AI Systems, and Game Systems Architecture. I love building modular, scalable systems that stay clean as a project grows and feel great in the player’s hands.

Programming is my main focus, but I like to wear many hats. I’ve shipped games with small teams, worked across most of the pipeline, including animation, VFX/SFX, and production, and stepped into leadership as both a Technical Lead and a Producer. I’m happy to jump in wherever a team needs me.

My proudest achievement so far is leading the programming team on my capstone project, Maid Mayhem. We showcased it at the Level Up Student Showcase 2026, where it took home two awards: 1st in People’s Choice, voted on by the public and presented by Ubisoft Toronto, and 2nd in Achievement in Audio, judged by industry professionals and presented by Behaviour Interactive. Our team was also invited to showcase at XP Game Summit 2026.

What I Work With

  • Gameplay programming in C# (Unity), focused on systems that are clean, scalable, and responsive
  • Data structures, algorithms, and CS fundamentals that shape how I approach performance and problem-solving
  • Real-time graphics and simulation programming in C++, from rendering pipelines to the math behind transforms and movement
  • Networked and mobile game development, where latency, memory, and performance constraints really count
  • Game AI, including finite state machines (FSMs), plus an introduction to machine learning and genetic algorithms
