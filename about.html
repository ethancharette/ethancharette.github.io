It's me!

Hi, I’m Ethan, a game developer from the Niagara Region, and thanks for checking out my portfolio!
I grew up playing games with my siblings and my parents, and those fond memories are what pushed me into game development. Now I’ve got two games out on Steam, a goofy office sim and an award-winning party brawler.

my resume ↗

My main experience lies in Gameplay Programming, AI Systems, and Game Systems Architecture. That being said, I love all aspects of game development! I especially love building modular, scalable systems that stay clean as a project grows and feel great in the player’s hands.

Programming is my main focus, but I like to wear many hats. I’ve shipped games with small teams and have worked in many different areas, including animation, VFX/SFX, and production. I've stepped into leadership as both a Technical Lead and a Team Lead, and I’m happy to jump in wherever a team needs me!

My proudest achievement so far has got to be leading the programming team on my capstone project, Maid Mayhem. We showcased it at the Level Up Student Showcase 2026, where it took home two awards: 1st in People’s Choice, voted on by the public and presented by Ubisoft Toronto, and 2nd in Achievement in Audio, judged by industry professionals and presented by Behaviour Interactive. Our team was also invited to showcase at XP Game Summit 2026, which was a great opportunity to meet more of the community.

When I’m not making games I’m probably playing them, and when I’m not playing them I guess I'm touching grass or something. Minecraft is certainly an all-time classic, but my Steam library is huge: Subnautica, Baldur’s Gate, Expedition 33, Core Keeper, Darkest Dungeon, What Remains of Edith Finch. I recently played Big Walk and my oh my the puzzle design is unreal.
Outside of games I play the drums and spend lots of time watching One Piece.

I love debugging, and I love watching a new player react to something I helped build. On a team I try to be dependable and goal-oriented. I’m hoping to land with people who think completely differently than I do, because that’s how I feel I learn best.
